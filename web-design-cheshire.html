Web design for small businesses in Cheshire

Pixel North is based in Northwich, and Cheshire is where we meet clients in person. It isn't the limit of who we work with: we build websites for small businesses anywhere in the UK.

In Cheshire

We come to you. James is based in Northwich, so a chat at your shop, workshop, studio or café is easy to arrange, and the coffee's on James.

Anywhere in the UK

Not in Cheshire? You get the same service by phone, video call and email: the same designer, the same plain-English process and the same clear written quotation.

Why meet in person?

When we're in Cheshire, we can see your business first-hand: your premises, your products and the way customers find you. It helps us build a site that feels like you, and it makes that first conversation quicker. Meeting in person is a bonus, not a requirement.

We're happy to meet across the county, from Northwich and Winsford to Chester, Knutsford, Macclesfield and Crewe. If you're not sure whether we can get to you, just ask.

What we can do for your business

  • Website design and build. A custom, mobile-friendly site that looks sharp and says clearly what you do, from first sketch to launch.
  • Website refresh. A tired or confusing site, tidied up, with a fresher design and a simpler structure.
  • Content and copy support. Help shaping your pages, from structure to plain-English wording.
  • Search and analytics setup. The basics that help people find you: search foundations, your Google Business Profile and simple analytics.
  • Enquiry forms and bookings. Contact forms and enquiry or booking tools that send customers straight to you.
  • Care and support. Hosting, backups, updates and small edits, so your site stays safe and up to date after launch.

There's more on each of these in our services.

Being found locally

If you serve customers in Cheshire, being easy to find nearby matters. We set up the search foundations of your site and can help with your Google Business Profile, so people looking for what you do can find you and get in touch.

How it works

  1. An initial consultation. We talk about your business, your goals and what you need from a website, in person if you're in Cheshire, or by phone or video call.
  2. A clear quotation. You get a written quotation covering what's included, the timeline and the cost.
  3. Start when you're ready. No pressure. If it looks right, we get going. If not, you've lost nothing.
