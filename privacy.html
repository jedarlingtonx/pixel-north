Privacy notice

Last updated 25 October 2026

Who I am

This website is run by J E Darlington Holdings Limited (company number 16490976), trading as Pixel North, a freelance web design business serving small businesses in Cheshire. J E Darlington Holdings Limited is responsible for your personal data. You can contact the owner at jedarlington@live.co.uk.

What this site collects

The pages themselves don’t store personal data. With your consent, the site uses Google Analytics cookies to count visits and see how the site is used. If you say no, those cookies aren’t set. A cookie that remembers your choice is always on. See the cookie policy for details, and you can change your choice at any time using “Cookie settings” in the footer.

If you fill in the contact form, the details you enter (your first and last name, email address, business name, what you need, and your message) are sent through a form service, Formspree, which emails them to me. I use them to reply to your enquiry.

Other companies involved

  • Google Fonts. The site loads its fonts from Google, so when a page loads your browser contacts Google and your IP address is shared with it. See Google’s privacy policy.
  • GitHub Pages. The site is hosted by GitHub, which may record technical information, such as IP addresses, when pages are requested. See GitHub’s privacy statement.
  • Google Analytics. If you accept analytics cookies, Google Analytics collects information about how you use the site, such as the pages you view, your approximate location, and your device and browser type. See how Google uses data from sites that use its services.
  • Formspree. Contact form submissions pass through Formspree, a form service based in the United States, which sends them to me by email and keeps a copy for a limited period. See Formspree’s privacy policy.
  • Email. Enquiries are handled through my email provider.

How I use your details

I use your details to reply to your enquiry, discuss your project, and prepare a quotation. The legal basis is that this is necessary to take steps at your request before entering into a contract, or otherwise in my legitimate interests in running my business.

I don’t sell your details or use them for unrelated marketing. I only share them with service providers that help me run my business, such as my email provider.

I use analytics data only to understand and improve the site. The legal basis is your consent, which you can withdraw at any time through “Cookie settings”.

How long I keep them

If you don’t become a client, I keep your enquiry for up to 12 months and then delete it. If you do become a client, I keep records for as long as needed to do the work and to meet legal and accounting requirements, which is usually up to six years.

Analytics data is kept in Google Analytics for up to 14 months. Formspree keeps its own copy of form submissions for a limited period (up to 30 days on its free plan).

Transfers outside the UK

Some of the companies above, including Formspree, are based in the United States or may process data outside the UK. Where they do, I rely on the protections they put in place, as required by UK data protection law.

Your rights

You can ask me to show you the personal data I hold about you, correct it, delete it, restrict how I use it, or stop using it. In some cases you can also ask for it in a portable format. To do any of these, email me at the address above.

If you’re unhappy with how I’ve handled your data, you can complain to the Information Commissioner’s Office at ico.org.uk. I’d appreciate the chance to put things right first.

Changes to this notice

If I change this notice, I’ll update the date at the top of the page.
